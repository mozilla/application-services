listCandidatesByOrigin
Link copied to clipboard
open override fun listCandidatesByOrigin(origins: List<String>, domains: List<String>): List<LoginCandidate>

Like list_candidates(), but only the logins whose origin is one of origins, or whose host is one of domains or a subdomain of one. Meant as a pre-filter for consumers with their own origin matching: the result is a superset of what they match, as long as domains holds the base domain (eTLD+1, which the caller computes) of every host they accept subdomains of.
